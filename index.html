What are divergence signals?

A divergence happens when Bitcoin makes a new price extreme (higher high or lower low), while Fear & Greed does not make a new extreme (lower high or higher low). It can signal that the trend is losing momentum.

5% and 5-point thresholds: For a BUY divergence, Bitcoin must open at least 5% below its reference clump's lowest daily open, while Fear & Greed must be at least 5 points above its lowest reading. For a SELL divergence, Bitcoin must reach at least 5% above whichever is higher: the reference SELL clump's highest BTC daily open or the prior confirmed macro-cycle ATH. Fear & Greed must be at least 5 points below the reference clump's highest reading. SELL divergences also require 14 consecutive BTC daily opens above that macro ATH.

2-day confirmation: The required BTC price and Fear & Greed conditions must be met for two consecutive days before a divergence signal appears.

Signal continuation: Once confirmed, the divergence signal continues to flash each day until the required conditions are no longer met.

What is a clump?

A clump begins with its first regular BUY or SELL signal and includes the strong signals that follow within a small time period. A clump ends after 7 consecutive days of no strong signals. A recent clump can be used as a reference for future divergence signals.

For BUY divergences, the reference uses the lowest BTC daily opening price and the lowest Fear & Greed reading within the clump, even if they occur on different days.

For SELL divergences, the reference uses the highest BTC daily opening price and the highest Fear & Greed reading within the clump, even if they occur on different days.

Details & restrictions

1. Regular and strong signals
  • Regular signals flash on day 2 only of a qualifying F&G streak; strong signals flash on day 3 and each following qualifying day.
  • A day outside the F&G threshold ends the streak.
  • Regular BUY only: blocked if any SELL signal occurred in the prior 60 days. Strong and divergence BUY signals are exempt.
  • All SELL signals: require 14 consecutive BTC opens above the prior confirmed macro-cycle high.
2. Clumps and divergence reference selection

What is a clump? A clump is a group of BUY signals or SELL signals that occur close together in time. Signals of the same type are grouped together when fewer than 7 days separate them. A gap of 7 days ends the clump. The clump's most extreme BTC price and Fear & Greed values are used to calculate potential BUY and SELL divergence signals.

Reference selection rules

  • Eligible clump days: Only days with regular or strong BUY or SELL signals are used to determine a clump's reference values. Days without signals are excluded.
  • BUY divergence reference clump: The previous BUY clump with the lowest BTC opening price is used as the reference for BUY divergences. The comparison uses the lowest BTC open and lowest Fear & Greed reading from that clump.
  • SELL divergence reference clump: The previous SELL clump with the highest BTC opening price is used as the reference for SELL divergences. The comparison uses the highest BTC open and highest Fear & Greed reading from that clump.
  • Selecting values in a reference clump: The lowest BTC opening price and Fear & Greed reading are used for BUY clumps, and the highest for SELL clumps. These values may occur on different days within the same clump. If a newer clump matches the BTC price extreme of the existing reference clump, the existing reference clump is retained.
  • Updating divergence references: The indicator checks for a divergence using the current reference clump before considering a newer clump as its replacement. This prevents a new reference from affecting a divergence that is already forming.
3. Divergence confirmation and continuation
  • BUY divergence: BTC opens ≥5% below the selected clump's BTC low and F&G is ≥5 points above that same clump's F&G low. BTC must also be below the preceding BUY-clump low, if one exists.
  • SELL divergence: BTC opens ≥5% above the selected SELL-clump BTC high and F&G is ≥5 points below that clump's F&G high.
  • Both price and F&G conditions must qualify for 2 consecutive completed days. The original reference is frozen during confirmation; the first divergence flashes on day 2.
  • After confirmation, a divergence flashes only on days its conditions hold. A gap of 1–6 days preserves the frozen reference but produces no signal on nonqualifying days. After 7 signal-free days, reset the reference.
  • Divergence invalidation & reset: A BUY divergence reference is invalidated if Fear & Greed falls strictly below its reference clump low, even before a divergence flashes, and vice versa for SELL divergences. Matching the extreme does not invalidate it. If a divergence was already flashing, its signal clump becomes the new reference. Otherwise, a new qualifying signal clump is required. Any subsequent divergence requires a fresh two-day confirmation.
4. Eligibility, macro cycles, and data

Macro-cycle tops: A potential top is tracked once BTC records 30 consecutive daily opens above the previous confirmed top. The tracked level rises with each new opening high. A new macro-cycle top is confirmed only after 30 consecutive opens above the previous top, a 15% decline from the highest opening price, and 365 days without a new opening high.

Macro-cycle bottoms: Starting 270 days after a confirmed macro high, a daily open must be strictly below every earlier post-peak daily open to establish a potential low. Each lower open updates the candidate. One subsequent daily open above that macro high confirms the low.

Historical data: BTC daily opening prices come from Coinbase Exchange. The chart begins in February 2018, but its macro-cycle calculations use a December 17, 2017 historical peak of $19,475.80 from Yahoo Finance as their starting reference.

  • Clump references expire after 18 months. A newer strong-SELL phase invalidates an older BUY-divergence setup.
  • A new macro-cycle top requires 30 consecutive opens above the prior confirmed top, followed by a 15% opening-price drawdown and 365 days without a new opening high. Confirmation never changes earlier SELL signals retroactively.
  • Missing values are not estimated. F&G streaks are counted independently of BTC availability, but plotting a signal requires a valid BTC open.
  • Known gaps: BTC September 4, 2020; F&G April 14–16, 2018 and October 26, 2024.
