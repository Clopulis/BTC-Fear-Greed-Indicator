What are divergence signals?

A divergence happens when Bitcoin makes a new price extreme (higher high or lower low), while Fear & Greed does not make a new extreme (lower high or higher low). It can signal that the trend is losing momentum.

5% and 5-point thresholds: For a BUY divergence, Bitcoin must close at least 5% below its reference clump's lowest daily close, while Fear & Greed must be at least 5 points above its lowest reading. For a SELL divergence, Bitcoin must close at least 5% above its reference clump's highest daily close, while Fear & Greed must be at least 5 points below its highest reading.

2-day confirmation: The required BTC price and Fear & Greed conditions must be met for two consecutive days before a divergence signal appears.

Signal continuation: Once confirmed, the divergence signal continues to flash each day until the required conditions are no longer met.

What is a clump?

A clump begins with its first regular BUY or SELL signal and includes the strong signals that follow within a small time period. A clump ends after 7 consecutive days of no strong signals. A recent clump can be used as a reference for future divergence signals.

For BUY divergences, the reference uses the lowest BTC daily closing price and the lowest Fear & Greed reading within the clump, even if they occur on different days.

For SELL divergences, the reference uses the highest BTC daily closing price and the highest Fear & Greed reading within the clump, even if they occur on different days.

Details & restrictions

1. Regular and strong signals
  • Regular signals flash on day 2 only of a qualifying F&G streak; strong signals flash on day 3 and each following qualifying day.
  • A day outside the F&G threshold ends the streak.
  • Regular BUY only: blocked if any SELL signal occurred in the prior 60 days. Strong and divergence BUY signals are exempt.
  • All SELL signals: require 14 consecutive BTC closes above the prior confirmed macro-cycle high.
2. Clumps and reference selection
  • A clump includes its initial regular BUY or SELL signal and the same-direction strong signals that follow. It ends after 7 consecutive days without a strong signal; shorter gaps remain part of the clump.
  • BUY: reference the eligible clump with the lowest BTC close, paired with the lowest F&G from that clump.
  • SELL: reference the eligible clump with the highest BTC close, paired with the highest F&G from that clump.
  • The initial regular BUY or SELL signal is included in its clump and can set either the BTC closing-price extreme or the F&G extreme. Later strong-signal days can also set either extreme.
  • BTC and F&G reference extremes may occur on different dates. A price tie favors the most recent clump; an F&G tie favors the latest eligible day.
  • Active clumps update their extremes. Test a divergence against the prior reference before switching to a new clump with a more extreme BTC close.
3. Divergence confirmation and continuation
  • BUY: BTC closes ≥5% below the selected clump's BTC low and F&G is ≥5 points above that same clump's F&G low. BTC must also be below the preceding BUY-clump low, if one exists.
  • SELL: BTC closes ≥5% above the selected SELL-clump BTC high and F&G is ≥5 points below that clump's F&G high.
  • Both price and F&G conditions must qualify for 2 consecutive completed days. The original reference is frozen during confirmation; the first divergence flashes on day 2.
  • After confirmation, a divergence flashes only on days its conditions hold. A gap of 1–6 days preserves the frozen reference but produces no signal on nonqualifying days. After 7 signal-free days, reset the reference.
4. Eligibility, macro cycles, and data

The dashed grey macro-cycle line begins after 14 consecutive closes above the previous confirmed cycle high, then rises with new closing highs and stays at the peak.

  • Clump references expire after 18 months. A newer strong-SELL phase invalidates an older BUY-divergence setup.
  • A new macro-cycle top requires 30 consecutive closes above the prior confirmed top, followed by a 15% closing-price drawdown and 365 days without a new closing high. Confirmation never changes earlier SELL signals retroactively.
  • Missing values are not estimated. F&G streaks are counted independently of BTC availability, but plotting a signal requires a valid BTC close.
  • Known gaps: BTC September 4, 2020; F&G April 14–16, 2018 and October 26, 2024.
